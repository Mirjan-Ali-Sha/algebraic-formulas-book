# Unified Algebraic Formulas and Proofs

A visual, step-by-step algebra book for grades 6–12, unifying every major formula and identity from basic squares to binomial theorem, exponentials, logarithms, and real-world modelling. [file:1]

---

## Who this book is for

- Students who want a **single, coherent algebra book** instead of many fragmented levels.  
- Teachers and tutors who want **visual-first explanations** and fully worked proofs they can adapt.  
- Self-learners, including arts-oriented and “non-math” learners, who need **clear diagrams, patterns, and stories** rather than just rules. [file:1]

Every topic is presented with:

- A simple **story or motivation**.  
- A **visual picture** (geometry, area, blocks, or graphs).  
- A clean **algebraic proof** with step-by-step reasoning.  
- **Worked examples** and graded exercises (Mild / Medium / Spicy / Extra Hot).  
- Short **answers**, with full solutions collected in the Answers & Reference section.

---

## How the book is organised

### Part 1 – Core Foundations (Ch. 1–5)

Introduces the language of algebra:

- What algebra is and why formulas are useful.  
- Variables, patterns, and expressions.  
- Visualising simple equations and core identities.  
- Squares of sums and differences, and the difference of squares identity. [file:1]

### Part 2 – Expanding Algebraic Thinking (Ch. 6–11)

Builds richer expression skills:

- Product and factorisation formulas.  
- Three-variable and cubic identities.  
- Patterns in expansions (Pascal’s triangle, binomial coefficients).  
- Higher-power expressions and special factorisations.  
- Quadratic equations: roots, factorisation, and the quadratic formula. [file:1]

### Part 3 – Equations and Progressions (Ch. 12–20)

Develops sequences, series, and exponent tools:

- Arithmetic, geometric, and other sequence types.  
- Finite and infinite series, with visual interpretations.  
- Properties of exponents and basic surds/radicals.  
- (Reserved chapters for your additional equation/progression topics). [file:1]

### Part 4 – Advanced Structures and Functions (Ch. 21–30)

Connects identities to deeper algebraic structures:

- Binomial theorem: introduction, visual/combinatorial proofs, symbolic proofs.  
- Polynomials and factorisation beyond quadratics.  
- Exponential and logarithmic functions as inverse partners.  
- Transformations and compositions of functions.  
- Advanced symmetric identities and inequalities.  
- Introductory functional equations and mixed advanced practice. [file:1]

### Part 5 – Applications, Modelling, and Integration (Ch. 31–35)

Shows algebra at work in real contexts:

- Linear and quadratic modelling from stories to formulas.  
- Exponential and logarithmic models of growth, decay, and scientific scales.  
- Sequences and series in savings, loans, and repeated processes.  
- Cross-discipline applications in physics, chemistry, finance, and art.  
- Capstone projects and a unified formula toolbox. [file:1]

## Answers, glossary, and formula sheets

The **Answers & Reference** section (sidebar link) includes:

- `solutions_part1`–`solutions_part5`: full, step-by-step solutions to chapter exercises.  
- `glossary`: concise definitions of all key terms (e.g., binomial coefficient, discriminant, half-life, asymptote).  
- `formula-sheet`: a compact “toolbox” of core identities and models, grouped by topic, with brief meaning cues. [file:1]

These are intended for revision and self-checking. Learners are encouraged to **attempt exercises first**, then use the solutions and formula sheet to confirm understanding.

## How to navigate this GitHub Pages site

- Use the **left sidebar** (Material theme) to jump between parts and chapters.  
- Use the **search box** (top-right) to find keywords like “binomial,” “quadratic,” or “pH.”  
- Each chapter page follows a consistent structure:
  - Introduction & motivation  
  - Visual/shape-based proof or intuition  
  - Algebraic proof and formulas  
  - Worked examples  
  - Exercises (Mild → Extra Hot)  
  - Short answers (with full solutions in the Answers section)

For offline reading or printing:

- Use your browser’s **“Print to PDF”** on any chapter or on the formula sheet.  
- MkDocs Material also supports good print styling, so sections will paginate cleanly.
